The founder brings hands-on experience supporting business-critical voice and unified communications environments, with a focus on structured troubleshooting, evidence-led root-cause investigation, and practical technical handovers. Approach

The core specialism is investigating difficult VoIP issues: following SIP signalling and call flows, reviewing RTP and SDP behaviour, analysing packet captures and logs, and narrowing down problems across session border controllers, PBX platforms, firewalls, NAT, and supporting infrastructure. Linux, networking, virtualisation, and monitoring knowledge help distinguish application, media, network, and infrastructure causes. The focus is on troubleshooting and operational support rather than claiming to architect every underlying platform from scratch.
